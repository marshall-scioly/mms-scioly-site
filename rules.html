Before You Register

Registration Rules & Information

Please read these guidelines carefully before registering for the 2026–2027 Science Olympiad season. Registering means you understand and agree to everything below.

The Season & Commitment

This registration is for Marshall Middle School students who want to join the Science Olympiad team for the 2026–2027 season.

This is a real commitment. Science Olympiad is a team event. If you don't believe your student can do full justice to the program — attending the weekly classes and competing at Regionals — now, before you register, is the time to step back. Once a student is on the team, not showing up affects the performance and standing of the entire team.
  • Regional competition: early February, always on a Saturday — the exact date will be announced. Participation is mandatory for all assigned team members.
  • Coaching sessions: run from September through the competition. Attendance at all coaching sessions for your assigned events is required.
  • Scheduling: please review the coaching schedule before choosing events, and don't pick events that conflict with your existing commitments (sports, music lessons, other classes, etc.).
Important: Submitting the registration form and paying the fee does not guarantee a spot on the team. Spots are limited.

Registration Fee

The team fee is $200, paid securely by PayPal on our Team Dues page. A PayPal account isn't required — credit and debit cards are accepted.

The fee helps cover facility rentals, team and national/regional registration fees, event supplies, coaching materials, and team T-shirts for students and coaches.

Refunds: if your student is not placed on the team, your registration fee will be refunded.

Financial hardship? No student is turned away for inability to pay. Please email scioly@mmofoundation.com to discuss options before registering.

How to Register

  1. Pay the $200 fee via PayPal on the Team Dues page.
  2. PayPal will email you a confirmation with a Transaction ID — keep that email.
  3. Complete the registration form with your contact information, event choices, and your PayPal Transaction ID.

One form per student. Duplicate submissions for the same student will all be considered void, so please submit only once.

Submissions are final. Please take your time and review your answers — no changes are accepted after you submit.

Registration dates for the 2026–2027 season will be announced soon.

Team Placement

We can only admit a limited number of students to the team. Registering and paying does not guarantee placement. When spots are limited, they are assigned in the following order:

  1. Prior placement and/or experience with Science Olympiad (applies to 7th and 8th graders)
  2. Coaches' children
  3. Grade level (8 > 7 > 6)
  4. First come, first served for 6th graders (except 6th graders whose parents are coaches)

If your student is not assigned a spot on the team, your registration fee will be refunded.

A note for coaching families: Being a coach's child does not guarantee that the student will be assigned the same event their parent coaches. We will make every effort to place the student in that event if they are interested in it, but all event assignments follow the same balancing process.

Event Selection & Assignment

Before ranking your events, review the coaching schedule and the event guide.

  • Rank your top 10 events in order (1 = most wanted).
  • Your top 10 must include at least 2 build events: Boomilever, Elastic Launch Glider, Hovercraft, Ping Pong Parachute, Roller Coaster, and Scrambler.
  • Each student is placed in 3 to 4 events (3 minimum, 4 maximum).
  • We aim to give every student a balanced mix of study, lab/hybrid, and build events.
  • We try our best to honor your top-10 choices, but assignments are not guaranteed. In rare cases we may assign an event outside your top 10 to keep the team balanced.
  • You are given the option to select up to 3 events that your student absolutely cannot do. These must be events not already in your top 10, and you'll provide a brief reason for each.
Once team formation is complete, no requests for event changes are accepted.

Partners

If you would like to be paired with a specific partner, enter their name (and email) on the registration form. They must enter your student's name on their form as well. You should both submit an identical top-10 list in the same order. If you do not choose a partner, we will match you with one.

Code of Conduct

Every team member and family is expected to represent Marshall Middle School with respect, integrity, and good citizenship — at practices, online, and at competitions.

Students are expected to:

  • Attend all coaching sessions for their assigned events, arrive on time, and come prepared. It is your responsibility to show up on time for all classes and to let your coaches know beforehand if you have an unavoidable conflict.
  • Treat coaches, teammates, volunteers, competitors, and event officials with respect.
  • Demonstrate good sportsmanship and academic integrity — no cheating, plagiarism, or breaking event rules.
  • Take care of shared tools, materials, and facilities.

Parents/guardians are expected to:

  • Support their student's attendance and commitment for the full season.
  • Get their student to and from practices and competitions on time.
  • Communicate respectfully with coaches and the admin team.

Consequences

Behavior that disrupts the team, violates Science Olympiad rules, or repeatedly fails the attendance commitment (such as unexcused absences or chronic lateness) may result in a warning, loss of event assignments, or removal from the team, at the discretion of the admin team. Registration fees are not refunded for students who leave the team or are removed for conduct or attendance reasons.

State Competition

If the school is selected to send a team to the State Competition, student selection is done solely by the admin team based on multiple factors, including performance, citizenship, and the best interests of the team.

Questions?

Email us any time at scioly@mmofoundation.com — we're happy to help.

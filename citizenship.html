Team Citizenship Expectations

Every student and family on the team is expected to read and follow these expectations for the season.

Attendance & Punctuality

  • Attendance is mandatory. Report any absence to your coach in advance. No more than two absences are allowed per season.
    • If there are special or extenuating circumstances, you must convey them in writing to scioly@mmofoundation.com.
  • Be on time. Classes and practices begin at the scheduled time.

Meeting Conduct

  • Come prepared with your notes, supplies, and materials, and stay focused on your event.
  • No phone use during meetings.
  • Everyone in this program, coaches included, is a volunteer who puts in real time and effort to keep it running. Be mindful of that, and be respectful.
  • Do not touch materials or work belonging to teachers, coaches, or other students.
  • Leave everything as you found it. This applies to classrooms, labs, coaches' homes and garages, and any other facility you use.

Online Classes

  • Turn on your camera during Zoom classes and online tests.
  • Be in a quiet space, free of distractions (no siblings, TV, etc.).
  • Stay muted unless a coach asks you a question or you have one to ask.
  • No off-topic or distracting chat messages.

Safety & Equipment

  • Follow all safety instructions during build events, labs, or any activity involving tools.
  • Wear required safety equipment (goggles, gloves, etc.) when instructed.
  • Handle team materials and equipment with care, and report damage or loss to your coach promptly.
  • Tell your coach right away if you or a teammate gets hurt, even a minor injury, so it can be handled properly.

Integrity & Sportsmanship

  • Do your own work, and follow Science Olympiad rules on what help is and is not allowed during events.
  • Don't share, discuss, or post details of competition tests, problems, or answers with anyone, including teammates who haven't taken that event yet. This is a Science Olympiad rule, not just a team one.
  • Demonstrate good sportsmanship and citizenship at all times. All Marshall Middle School rules and policies apply to Science Olympiad too.

Conduct & Inclusion

  • Bullying, harassment, or excluding a teammate for any reason will not be tolerated. This applies in person and online.
  • If you post about the team on social media, be respectful of teammates and coaches, and do not share others' photos or videos without their okay.
  • No alcohol, drugs, tobacco, or vaping at any practice, competition, or team event.

Communication

  • Check your email and Google Classroom regularly for updates, assignments, and communication from your coach.

Competition Day

  • Arrive on time with your required materials and binders.
  • Follow the instructions of venue staff and judges.
  • No arguing with judges or event supervisors. If you think there's a scoring or rules issue, bring it to the Marshall Science Olympiad admin, only they can file a protest.
  • Follow whatever electronics policy is posted at each competition (many events ban phones or calculators during trials).

Parents

  • Drop off and pick up on time, and follow parking regulations.
  • Be prepared to help the coach set up and clean up for in-person classes.
  • Respond promptly to all Science Olympiad communications, including tournament logistics and travel coordination for off-site events.

Consequences

A first violation typically gets a conversation with your coach. Repeated or serious violations may result in loss of an event assignment or removal from the program, decided with the coach or the admin team.

Amendments

Coaches may update these expectations during the season. Changes will be posted on this page.

Questions? Email us any time at scioly@mmofoundation.com.

Register for the Season

Join the Marshall Middle School Science Olympiad team for 2026–2027.

Registration opens soon

Student registration for the 2026–2027 season isn't open yet — dates will be announced shortly. Join the email list to be the first to know when it goes live.

Before you register

The team fee is $200, paid by PayPal on the Team Dues page (a PayPal account isn't required — cards are accepted). The fee is refunded only if your student is not placed on the team. Please read the full registration rules first — registering means you understand and agree to them.

How registration will work

  1. Pay the $200 fee via PayPal on the Team Dues page, and keep the confirmation email with your PayPal Transaction ID.
  2. Complete the registration form (it will be linked here once it opens) with your contact information, your top event rankings, and your Transaction ID.
  3. Watch your email for placement news. Paying and submitting does not guarantee a spot — team size is limited and placement is decided by the admin team.
The registration form will appear here when it opens. Check back soon, or join the email list for an alert the moment it's live.

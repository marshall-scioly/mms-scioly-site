Register for the Season

Getting ready to join the Marshall Middle School Science Olympiad team for 2026–2027.

1. Read the rules

Please read the full registration rules before you begin — registering means you understand and agree to them. They cover the season commitment, event selection, team placement, and refunds.

Read the Rules

2. Review the events and coaching schedule

Go through this year's events, then check the coaching schedule for practice days and times. Attendance at all coaching sessions is required, so choose events that fit around your student's schedule.

This Season's Events Coaching Schedule

3. Pay the team fee

The team fee is $200, paid by PayPal on the Team Dues page (a PayPal account isn't required — cards are accepted). The fee is refunded only if your student is not placed on the team. Keep the PayPal confirmation email — you'll enter your Transaction ID in the registration form.

Pay Team Dues

4. Fill out the registration form

Fill out the registration form once per student — it opens in a new tab. You'll need your PayPal Transaction ID from step 3 and your student's top event choices. Submissions are final — you won't be able to edit your answers after submitting, so please review everything before you send it.

Registration closes Saturday, August 22, 2026 at 12:00 noon PST. The form stops accepting responses at that time.

Questions: scioly@mmofoundation.com
